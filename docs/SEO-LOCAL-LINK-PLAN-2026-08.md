# Local Link & Citation Plan — Handy Services

_Created 12 Aug 2026. Owner: growth. Review monthly._

## Why this exists (the one-line case)

GSC (12 Aug) shows the problem clearly: **7,717 impressions/28d but only 92 clicks, average position 29.9 (page 3).** Demand is surging — `local handyman near me` +1,271%, `odd job man near me` +908% in two weeks — but we rank ~#26 for it, so it converts to almost nothing.

The Moz link file (11 Aug) explains the ceiling: **Domain Authority 3, 15 referring domains, 17 total backlinks.** That tiny, weak link profile is *why* Google shows the pages but won't rank them. The 24% Spam Score is noise off such a small profile — not the problem.

**The single highest-leverage SEO move right now is not more pages or on-page tweaks — it's building a handful of genuinely good local links + consistent citations.** That lifts authority *and* local relevance, which is exactly what "near me" ranking needs. It compounds with the near-me on-page work already shipped.

Target for the next 90 days: **15 → ~40 quality referring domains**, all NAP-consistent. Not link volume — link quality + local consensus.

## STEP 0 — Lock the canonical NAP (do this first, it gates everything)

Every citation below must use **identical** Name / Address / Phone / URL. Inconsistent NAP is worse than no citation — it splits Google's confidence in the business. Fill these in and never deviate:

| Field | Canonical value |
|---|---|
| **Name** | `Handy Services` (exactly — not "Handy Services Ltd", not "Handyman Nottingham") |
| **Address** | ⚠️ **TO CONFIRM** — a single consistent business address. As a service-area business you don't publish a street address to customers, but most citation sites require one. Use the registered/office address consistently, or a consistent city + postcode. Pick ONE and use it everywhere. |
| **Phone** | `+44 7449 501762` (one number, same format everywhere) |
| **Website** | `https://www.handyservices.app` |
| **Primary category** | Handyman / Handyman Service |
| **Service areas** | Nottingham + suburbs (Beeston, West Bridgford, Arnold, Carlton, Bulwell, Hucknall, Mapperley, Wollaton) |

> Decision needed from you: the business address to standardise on. Everything downstream depends on it.

These carry real trust signals, rank themselves for "handyman near me", and are what AI answer engines (ChatGPT/Perplexity) cite for local trades. Some are paid — treat as marketing spend with a link bonus, not pure SEO.

## WAVE 3 — Local relevance links (what actually cracks "near me")

These are the hardest and the most valuable — genuine local/editorial links tell Google we're *of* Nottingham, which is the missing proximity/authority signal behind the page-3 near-me positions.

- [ ] **Nottinghamshire Chamber of Commerce** — join / member directory link (local authority signal)
- [ ] **Local sponsorship** — a Nottingham grassroots sports club, school fair, or community event. Most publish a sponsors page with a link. £50–200 buys a genuine local editorial link + goodwill. Highest ROI local-link tactic for trades.
- [ ] **Supplier / merchant partner pages** — if we buy regularly from a local builders' merchant, tool hire, or trade supplier, ask to be listed as a trusted installer/partner.
- [ ] **Local news / PR** — a genuinely newsworthy angle (e.g. free work for a local cause, apprenticeship/academy launch — ties to the Contractor Academy) pitched to Nottingham Post / West Bridgford Wire / local community Facebook groups.
- [ ] **A linkable local resource** — publish one honest, referenceable data piece, e.g. _"What a handyman actually costs in Nottingham (2026)"_ using our real quote data. Local blogs and comparison sites link to concrete local data. Doubles as answer-engine (AEO) fuel.
- [ ] **Landlord/property-manager clients** — any client with a website (letting agents, property managers) → ask for a "maintenance by" credit link.

## What NOT to do (protects the profile)

- ❌ **No Fiverr/cheap "500 backlinks" packages, PBNs, or link exchanges.** *This* is what actually raises Spam Score and risks penalties — the opposite of the current harmless noise.
- ❌ **No inconsistent NAP.** One name, one address, one phone format. Everywhere.
- ❌ **Don't disavow the current 17 links.** At this size disavowing does more harm than good, and Google ignores junk automatically.
- ❌ **Don't chase volume.** 10 quality local links beat 200 directory dumps.

## Measurement & cadence

- **Monthly:** re-pull Moz (or Ahrefs/Semrush free) referring domains + DA. Track the count climbing 15 → 40.
- **Fortnightly:** GSC — watch average position on `handyman nottingham`, `local handyman near me`, `handyman near me`, `handyman derby`. Authority gains show as position (not just impression) improvement.
- **Definition of success:** near-me queries move from ~#26 toward page 1, and CTR rises off the 1.19% floor. Impressions we can't rank for don't count.

## Cloneable

This is the **per-city playbook** — when a new city launches (with a GBP + local contractor), run Waves 1–3 with that city's NAP + suburbs + local sponsorships. Same as the page-rollout gate: links follow delivery, not ahead of it.
