# Note to Ben: what's changing with customer messages

Hi Ben,

Quick heads-up on how customer messages are going to work over the next few weeks, and what you'll see.

**What happened.** On 31 August a new automated replier went live by mistake with none of the safety checks. It also stopped the normal one from working, so for two days nobody was answering WhatsApp messages. That's fixed. Right now the old assistant drafts replies and every one waits for you to approve it. Nothing goes to a customer without your tap.

**What we're building.** One reception desk that every message goes through. Simple things get a fixed reply straight away, any hour: "thanks, send us a photo and your postcode", "got the photos", "sorry we missed your call". Anything about price, dates, complaints or refunds comes to you and only you. Everything else, the AI drafts and you approve until it's proved it can be trusted on that type of reply.

**What you'll see in the queue.**
- **Drafts to approve.** A suggested reply with approve, edit and reject buttons. When you reject or edit, pick a reason from the short list. That's how the system learns, so please do use it.
- **Flags.** A customer question only you can answer, with a deadline on it. If the deadline passes the customer gets a "Ben's picking this up shortly" line so they aren't left hanging, and you get one more nudge.
- **Morning spot-checks.** A handful of messages that went out automatically overnight, with a "fine" or "not fine" button. Thirty seconds.

**Three things to keep doing.**
1. Reply to customers from the business number only, as you have been. The desk can only see that number.
2. Price every quote yourself. The system will pre-fill the lines and photos from the conversation or the call, but it never writes a number.
3. If a customer is unhappy, reply yourself. The system will ping you and hold them, but it won't apologise on your behalf or promise anything.

**What it's for.** A quarter of customer messages have been getting no reply at all, mostly evenings and weekends. This fixes that from week one, and by month two it should roughly halve the typing you do.

Courtnee will talk to you about hours for the queue, and what counts as work time. Nothing changes until that's agreed.

Any questions, ask Courtnee.
