# Contractor Academy — "The Standard" Module — Script, Shot List & Quiz

**Purpose:** Module 2. Teaches + certifies the Handy quality standard — the differentiator, and the score that feeds **ad-hoc → Core promotion**. Source of truth: `docs/JOB_STANDARD_AND_PHOTO_CHECKLIST.md` (the five standards + photo-proof checklist).

**Production:** photoreal **Seedance 2.0** silent b-roll (real job sites — this is where photoreal earns its keep) + Seed Audio narrator (same voice as the airplane intro) + ffmpeg overlays. NO AI talking-heads. Blue polo logo-free.

**Style key (reuse across blocks):** *"Real UK homes, natural light; a Handy contractor in a plain blue polo doing real work; each block a clean before/after or right-way/wrong-way pairing; premium, calm, documentary — not staged-cheesy."*

**Framing device:** each of the five standards = one right-way vs wrong-way pairing, then the photo-proof habit, then the promotion payoff. Target ~110s (11 blocks).

## Block-by-block

### Block 1 — The one line (0:00–0:10)
- **Narration:** "A Handy job is judged on five things. Miss any one and it's not done — no matter how good the rest is. The whole game: be excellent at everything a normal handyman does badly."
- **Visual:** Text builds the five words: *Finish · Clean · Communicate · Respect · Safe.*
- **On-screen:** `FIVE THINGS. EVERY TIME.`

### Block 2 — Right first time (0:10–0:20)
- **Narration:** "One. Right first time. The job that was quoted is fully done — no half-finished items, no 'I'll come back for that'."
- **Visual:** Wrong: a shelf up but one bracket loose, contractor walking off → Right: same shelf, every fixing checked, load-tested.
- **On-screen:** `1 · RIGHT FIRST TIME — no "I'll come back"`

### Block 3 — Scope changes (0:20–0:30)
- **Narration:** "If the scope changes on site, you agree it with ops *before* you carry on — not after. Ops sets price and scope. You deliver quality."
- **Visual:** Contractor spots extra work, stops, phones ops (opens app), gets the nod, then continues.
- **On-screen:** `SCOPE CHANGED? → OPS FIRST`

### Block 4 — Clean handover (0:30–0:40)
- **Narration:** "Two. Clean handover. You leave the area cleaner than you found it. Dust sheets down, debris gone, offcuts taken away, surfaces wiped. The customer never tidies up after you."
- **Visual:** Wrong: dust and offcuts left behind → Right: sheeted, hoovered, wiped, spotless.
- **On-screen:** `2 · CLEANER THAN YOU FOUND IT`

### Block 5 — On time / on communication (0:40–0:50)
- **Narration:** "Three. Turn up in the window. If you're running late or hit a problem, the customer *and* ops know before the slot — not after. A late arrival with warning is fine. A silent no-show never is."
- **Visual:** Wrong: clock past the window, phone silent → Right: contractor sends a heads-up text, customer relaxed.
- **On-screen:** `3 · LATE WITH WARNING ✓  ·  SILENT NO-SHOW ✗`

### Block 6 — Respectful in the home (0:50–1:00)
- **Narration:** "Four. Respect the home. Boots managed, no swearing, no smoking, careful around children, pets and tenants. In that house, you *are* the brand."
- **Visual:** Covers on at the door, polite nod, works cleanly around a child's toys and a cat.
- **On-screen:** `4 · YOU ARE THE BRAND`

### Block 7 — Safe and to spec (1:00–1:10)
- **Narration:** "Five. Safe and to spec — to a standard you'd accept in your own home. Anything you're not competent or qualified for is flagged to ops, never bodged."
- **Visual:** Wrong: a bodged, unsafe fix → Right: clean compliant work; then contractor flagging a gas job to ops instead of touching it.
- **On-screen:** `5 · SAFE & TO SPEC — never bodge`

### Block 8 — Photo proof, before/during (1:10–1:20)
- **Narration:** "Prove it. A wide 'before' of every work area. A close-up of any damage you did *not* cause — that protects you. And photograph anything hidden before you cover it back up."
- **Visual:** Phone-frame: wide before shot; close-up of pre-existing scratch; shot behind a panel.
- **On-screen:** `BEFORE · PRE-EXISTING DAMAGE · HIDDEN ISSUES`

### Block 9 — Photo proof, after (1:20–1:30)
- **Narration:** "Then the 'after' — same angle as the before, because the comparison *is* the proof. A close-up of the finish. And a wide shot showing it cleaned and tidied. No photos, no payment."
- **Visual:** Before/after slider on the same angle; close-up of a crisp finish; tidy wide shot.
- **On-screen:** `AFTER (same angle) · FINISH · TIDIED`

### Block 10 — Red-flag stops (1:30–1:40)
- **Narration:** "Four things mean stop and call ops first: scope bigger than quoted, a job needing a qualification you don't hold, anything unsafe, or a customer asking for something not on the sheet."
- **Visual:** Four red stop-cards flip in, one per trigger.
- **On-screen:** `STOP & CALL OPS: bigger scope · not qualified · unsafe · off-sheet`

### Block 11 — The promotion payoff (1:40–1:50)
- **Narration:** "Your first three jobs, a Handy lead checks your photos before payment. Clear them cleanly and you're on the standard flow — and on the path from ad-hoc to Core. Hit all five, every time, and the work comes to you."
- **Visual:** Three job cards stamp green; a progress bar fills toward "CORE".
- **On-screen:** `3 CLEAN JOBS → STANDARD FLOW → CORE`

## Quiz bank — "The Standard" (pass 80%, attempts logged, feeds promotion score)

**Q1 (Right first time, Block 2):** A job is 95% done but one item on the sheet is unfinished. It's:
- ✅ Not done — every quoted item must be complete or agreed with ops.
- Done enough if the customer seems happy.
- Done — you can note the last item for "next time".

**Q2 (Scope, Block 3):** You find extra work mid-job. You:
- Agree a price with the customer and crack on.
- ✅ Stop and clear the change with ops before proceeding.
- Do it free to keep them happy.

**Q3 (Clean handover, Block 4):** "Clean handover" means:
- Sweep up if you have time.
- ✅ Leave the area cleaner than you found it — debris gone, surfaces wiped.
- The customer tidies; you do the trade work.

**Q4 (Communication, Block 5):** You'll miss the arrival window. The right move:
- Turn up late and apologise in person.
- ✅ Tell the customer *and* ops before the slot.
- Say nothing if it's only 20 minutes.

**Q5 (Respect, Block 6):** In the customer's home you should:
- Work however's fastest; it's just a job.
- ✅ Manage boots, no swearing/smoking, mind children/pets/tenants.
- Only mind your manners if the customer's watching.

**Q6 (Safe & to spec, Block 7):** A task needs a qualification you don't hold:
- Do it carefully — you've seen it done.
- ✅ Flag it to ops; never bodge work outside your competence.
- Do it and photograph it well.

**Q7 (Photo proof, Blocks 8–9):** The "after" photo should be:
- Any angle that looks good.
- ✅ The same angle as the "before" — the comparison is the proof.
- Optional if the finish is obviously good.

**Q8 (Pre-existing damage, Block 8):** You spot a scratch you didn't cause. You:
- Ignore it — not your problem.
- Buff it out quietly.
- ✅ Photograph it before you start — it protects you and Handy.

**Q9 (Red-flag stop, Block 10):** Which does NOT require stopping to call ops first?
- Scope bigger than quoted.
- ✅ Choosing which of your own tools to use.
- Customer asks for something off the job sheet.

**Q10 (Promotion, Block 11):** On your first three jobs:
- Payment is automatic like any other job.
- ✅ A Handy lead reviews your photos before payment as a quality check.
- You're already a Core contractor.
