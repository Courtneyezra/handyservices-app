# Beliefs — what Handy Services actually stands for

These are the convictions underneath every piece of copy. If a post could have
been written by any handyman in the country, it hasn't used these.

## The core inversion
**Handy isn't a handyman. Handy is the standard a handyman should be — and
usually isn't.** We are excellent at everything a normal handyman does badly:
turning up, pricing straight, finishing properly, answering the phone.
Everything a handyman should be — and usually isn't.

## The accountability gap
A cheap handyman is a gamble. The cheap lone trader has nothing at stake — the
price gap between us and a random Gumtree ad is really an **accountability
gap**. We never position on being cheaper; we position on *someone is actually
on the hook*. £2M insured, DBS-checked, written quote, come-back-free
guarantee. Not a cowboy chasing a quick buck.

## Reliability is the headline promise
We turn up when we say — or your deposit back, no quibble. Not a faceless call
centre: real, vetted local pros. Reliability-per-promise beats everything else
we could brag about.

## The price we quote is the price
Fixed, in writing, before we start. No call-out fee, no hourly-rate surprises,
no "oh, that'll be extra". And you pay when it's done — balance only when
you're happy; we don't get paid until you are.

## Not right? We come back free.
The guarantee is always phrased as that question-then-answer. 90-day
workmanship guarantee, no arguments, no questions.

## Proof, not promises
Before-and-after photos on every job, photo report on completion, tax-ready
invoice same day. "You get photos so you can see the job was actually done."
Internally the rule is even blunter: no photos, no proof, no payment.

## Guest in the home
Boots off or covers on. Dust sheets, overshoes, spotless when we leave. Their
home, their rules — the customer's peace of mind is part of the job.

## Honesty over puffery
We deleted a fake "recent quotes" ticker on principle: real proof lives in
real reviews and real guarantees. We never claim credentials we don't hold,
never say 100%/cheapest/award-winning, and we tell customers honestly when a
job needs a specialist trade instead of us.

## People, not a company
Copy is signed by and about real people — Craig, Joe, Ben — never a faceless
"we" machine. A quote is "prepared by Ben"; a repeat customer hears "Craig's
already coming", never a "welcome" message. Sounds like a real person, not a
notification.

## Small jobs are the business
The wobbly shelf, the door that won't close, the flat-pack still in its box.
No job is too small to bother us with — clearing the whole list in one visit
is the product.
