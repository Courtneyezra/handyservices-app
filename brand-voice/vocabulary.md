# Vocabulary — the words we use and the words we ban

## Words that sound like us
sorted · proper / properly · turn up · pop round · price it up · no-fuss ·
tidy / spotless · knock out (a list) · dragging it out (what we don't do) ·
the whole list · one visit · fixed price · in writing · no quibble ·
vetted · on the hook · put it right · peace of mind · hands-off ·
"a bloke with a brush" (what a decorator isn't) · dodgy / cowboy (the
competition's worst, used sparingly)

## Canonical phrases (use verbatim or riff closely)
- "Not right? We come back and fix it free."
- "We turn up when we say — or your deposit back, no quibble."
- "The price we quote is the price."
- "Send a photo, get a fixed price back in minutes."
- "No waiting in, no call-out fee, a fixed price up front."
- "You don't need to be there." (landlord angle)
- "Whatever's up, we've sorted it before."
- "Everything a handyman should be — and usually isn't."

## Approved claims — the ONLY facts allowed
- Fixed price agreed up front — no surprises, no hidden extras
- No call-out fee
- £2M insured · DBS-checked and vetted
- 4.9★ on Google
- 90-day workmanship guarantee ("come back free")
- Photo report / before-and-after photos on completion
- Tax-ready invoice emailed same day
- Tenant coordination available · key collection available
- Full cleanup included
- Serving Nottingham & surrounds: West Bridgford, Beeston, Arnold, Sherwood,
  Mapperley, Wollaton…

## Banned outright
- Credentials we don't hold: certified, accredited, qualified, City & Guilds,
  NVQ, Gas Safe, NICEIC
- Puffery: 100%, money-back, cheapest, lowest price, unbeatable,
  award-winning, #1, best-in-class, world-class
- Availability lies: 24/7, round the clock, guaranteed same day, always
  available
- Scheduling ping-pong: "let me know when suits", "let us know what works",
  "shout to book"
- Vague list-trailing: "and more", "etc" — always enumerate the actual jobs
- Corporate filler: solutions, utilise, state-of-the-art, "we pride ourselves"
- Ending a post/headline on a bare "Sorted." / "Done." / "Job done."

## Spelling and style
UK English throughout (colour, metre, organise). £ prices, never GBP. Job
names in plain trade language: flat-pack, silicone/reseal, ironmongery,
skirting, architrave, guttering.
